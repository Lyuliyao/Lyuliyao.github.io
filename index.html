Research Interests 研究方向

Machine-learning methods for multiscale modeling (model reduction) 多尺度建模中的机器学习方法（模型降阶）

Many-body non-Markovian dissipative particle dynamics; state-dependent general Langevin dynamics.

多体非马尔可夫耗散粒子动力学；状态依赖的广义朗之万动力学。

Algorithms for scientific machine learning 科学机器学习算法

Mixed residual methods; deep-learning-based discontinuous Galerkin methods.

混合残差方法；基于深度学习的不连续 Galerkin 方法。

Gradient-free optimization 无梯度优化

Consensus-based optimization; consensus-based sampling.

基于共识的优化；基于共识的采样。
